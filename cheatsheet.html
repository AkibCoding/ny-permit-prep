Lights

  • Flashing red = STOP sign
  • Flashing yellow = caution
  • Steady yellow = about to turn red
  • Red + green arrow = go only that way, if clear
  • Red arrow = no turn that way
  • Light out = treat as STOP sign
  • Right on red after full stop, not in NYC
  • Left on red one-way to one-way only
  • Lane X: red = don't use · yellow = leave · flashing yellow = left turns

Lines

  • White = same direction · yellow = opposite
  • Broken = may pass if safe
  • Solid + broken = only the broken side passes
  • Double solid = no passing; cross only to turn left in/out
  • One solid = change lanes only if necessary
  • Edge line angles in = road narrows
  • Officer overrides everything

Who waits

  • Car already in the intersection goes first
  • Left-turner waits for oncoming
  • Same time at right angles: car on the left waits
  • Driveway or lot: stop and wait
  • Circle: wait for cars inside
  • Pedestrians in crosswalks; blind pedestrians always
  • Emergency vehicle any direction: pull right and stop

School bus

  • Yellow lights: slow, get ready to stop
  • Red lights: stop from both directions, even on a divided highway
  • At least 20 ft back; go when it moves or you're waved on
  • School buses stop at every railroad crossing, even empty

Alcohol

  • BAC depends on amount, speed, weight; not drink type
  • Only time lowers BAC
  • Implied Consent: refuse the test and lose your license
  • Cold medicine and tranquilizers can impair you

Emergencies

  • Don't panic
  • Blowout: hold wheel, ease off gas, no brakes yet
  • Brake failure: pump first
  • Stuck gas pedal: neutral + brake, don't turn off ignition
  • Rear skid: steer the way the rear slides
  • Head-on car: slow, go right, honk
  • Stalled on tracks: get out, go toward the train at 45°
  • In water: escape through a window
