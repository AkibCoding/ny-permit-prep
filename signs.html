Road signs

Every sign and what it means

The real test has 4 sign questions and you must get at least 2 right. Learn shapes and colors first; they tell you the type of sign even before you read it.

Shapes and colors

OctagonSTOP, always red
TriangleYIELD
Yellow diamondWarning: hazard ahead
OrangeWork zone
White rectangleA rule you must obey
GreenDirections and distances
BlueServices
Round yellowRailroad ahead

Meanings are hidden. Say the answer, then tap a sign to check.
