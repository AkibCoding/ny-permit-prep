About

Free, simple, and made to help you pass

NY Permit Prep started as one student's study notes for the Buffalo DMV permit test. It worked, so it became a free site for everyone.

What this site is

  • Free and open to everyone. No account, no email, no ads.
  • Based on the official NYS Driver's Manual (MV-21). Every question shows the manual page it comes from, so you can check it yourself.
  • Not the DMV. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles. Rules can change, so always check the official manual and DMV practice quizzes.
  • Questions are written for this site. They're worded like real test questions, but they are not copies of the DMV's test.

Privacy

Your quiz scores and missed questions are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data, or using "Reset my progress" on the home page, erases it.

Support this site

The site is free and will stay free. If it helped you pass and you'd like to say thanks, you can chip in toward hosting and updates. It's completely optional.

Found a mistake?

If a question or rule looks wrong or out of date, please report it on the project's GitHub page so it can be fixed for everyone.
